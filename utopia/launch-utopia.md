# Product Hunt / GitHub Launch Copy — Utopia

## Tagline
Op-shopper’s megastore of cool tech and desk-gear. Pay-by-invoice. Ships worldwide.

## Description
Utopia is PreCog Security’s dropship megastore for everyday tech: earbuds, mechanical keyboards, monitor light bars, fast chargers, desk mats and more. Every item is sourced to order, with a 30–40% margin and a transparent 24-hour PayPal invoice flow.

## First comment
We built Utopia as the first revenue surface while building PreCog Security’s AI studio. It is intentionally markdown-light: a static dev-free storefront designed to go from click-to-payment before talking to a supplier. Orders route through email + invoice; supplier procurement follows payment.
